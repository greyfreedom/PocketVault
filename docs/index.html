Designed with a boundary

Your master password stays the source of truth.

A random vault key encrypts your data. Your master password wraps that key, so changing the password is fast without compromising the encryption model.

01
Strong local derivation

PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with 600,000 iterations for current vault writes.

02
Authenticated encryption

Google Tink Streaming AEAD protects content and verifies integrity.

03
